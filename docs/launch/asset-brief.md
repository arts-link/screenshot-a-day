# v0.1.0 launch asset brief

Create assets from the released public demo only, with no private pages, credentials, administrator tokens, or personal data visible.

## Three core screenshots

1. Public gallery for `arts-link.com`, showing the capture history and browser labels.
2. Two-capture comparison, showing a clear but ordinary visual change.
3. Public gallery detail with a GIF or WebM timeline available.

Export a clean 16:9 version for README/directory use and retain the full-resolution originals. Add concise alt text describing the interface and the information conveyed.

## Walkthrough — 45 to 60 seconds

- 0–7s: state the problem over the public gallery: websites change, but uptime does not show how.
- 7–18s: move through dated captures and identify Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit profiles.
- 18–32s: compare two captures and show the overlay or heatmap.
- 32–43s: open the public sharing view and a GIF/WebM timeline.
- 43–52s: show the two-image Docker Compose quick start without displaying secrets.
- 52–60s: end on the Pages URL, demo, and “self-hosted · no product telemetry.”

Use captions, avoid background music that competes with narration, and verify the final frame remains readable on mobile.
