# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Screenshot-a-Day a harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, and sustained disruption. Report conduct concerns privately to the Arts-Link organization maintainers. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
